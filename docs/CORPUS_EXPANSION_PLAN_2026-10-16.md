# Inactive corpus expansion plan

User-authorized scope: Georgia-first university, foundation, credit-union, utility
and employer awards; selected regional/national awards available to Georgia
students; deeper official operational documents for existing programs.

The first collection inventory contains 37 explicit official URLs across 23
candidate families, in addition to the existing ten families. Collection does
not establish admission. Review fetched full text for an applicable cycle,
complete eligibility guidance, conflicts, actual operational content and readable
provenance. Expand the inventory with linked official rules and forms where they
add coverage. Historical/unpublished applications remain coverage leads and are
not silently promoted to current rules.

Aim for 30–40 reviewed families and 500–550 useful chunks, up to 750 only when
material justifies it. Counts are targets, not acceptance criteria. Never pad
with biographies, repeated text, multiple award-tier copies, unrelated policies,
or artificially short fragments. Report a shortfall when verified content cannot
justify the target.

Archive original bytes, final URLs, fetch receipts and normalized sections.
Review mixed pages by exact selected spans and record every exclusion. Preserve
tables, FAQ question/answer units and program boundaries. Pack adjacent related
short sections up to about 500 tokens with per-section evidence; split long
sections with at most 75 tokens overlap. Keep tiny but essential standalone
rules only with a recorded reason. Do not infer GPA scales, residency evidence,
acceptance, academic year or a guaranteed award.

Validate schemas, unique IDs, raw hashes, source offsets, evidence links, cycle
metadata, duplicate content, exclusion spans, token distribution and coverage
before paid work. Reconcile the one cumulative $5 ledger and outstanding paid
operations; verify current embedding pricing. Stage in a new namespace only,
read back metadata, verify readiness, and retain the old active version.

Hand the immutable candidate, source admission/deferral register, retained/new
program IDs, costs and limitations to Native build for evaluation. Activation
is excluded from this ingestion task.
