# Independent official-web audit of five demo answers — October 15, 2026

This audit compares the five already-observed app answers with freshly searched public pages from the scholarship administrators, universities and Georgia Student Finance Commission. The web search did not use the local ingestion corpus, Pinecone, or the RAG retrieval pipeline. Verdicts assess whether the answer addressed the fictional question, not whether its citations resolved inside the app. Public pages may change before the recorded demo.

| Case | Verdict | Independent official-source check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Georgia Southern | **Core facts correct; tier not determined.** | [Georgia Southern's Fall 2027 first-year merit page](https://www.georgiasouthern.edu/admissions-aid/tuition-and-scholarships/types-of-aid/scholarships) says one-time/non-renewable, completed admission by February 1, 2027, and tiers use the university's recalculated core GPA. A 3.60 transcript GPA cannot establish the $500 tier without that recalculation. The app showed the tier row, but its gap withheld a personal tier conclusion. |
| Park | **Facts correct, answer incomplete.** | [Park's application page](https://park.ncsu.edu/apply/) says 3.80 unweighted and non-rounded, separate NC State and Park applications by 11:59 p.m. ET November 1, and two Park recommendations. Its [FAQ](https://park.ncsu.edu/faq/) says recommendations are due November 1. Thus 3.79 cannot simply be rounded up to 3.80; the app quoted the rule but did not plainly say no. The public GPA rule gives no numeric scale, so the app's structured-match caution is defensible. |
| Coolidge | **Contextually incorrect/misleading.** | [Coolidge eligibility](https://coolidgescholars.org/eligibility/) says the 2026–27 application is for current juniors planning first full-time undergraduate enrollment in Fall 2028, subject to citizenship/residency and other conditions. The app's leading “No, only high school juniors...” fragment comes from an exception FAQ and can falsely signal that this fictional junior is ineligible. [Coolidge's timeline](https://coolidgescholars.org/selection-timeline/) confirms December 1, 2026 at 5 p.m. Pacific for all materials. Its [FAQ](https://coolidgescholars.org/about/faq/) confirms two letters; the app omitted that requested count. |
| Alabama vs. Auburn | **Facts cited correctly, but materially incomplete.** | [Alabama's 2027 nonresident chart](https://afford.ua.edu/scholarships/oos-international-freshman/) requires ACT 27 from one test date and GPA 3.50+ for Capstone; a 27 superscore alone does not meet that score rule. [Auburn's score page](https://www.auburn.edu/admissions/testscores.php) accepts ACT superscoring for scholarship consideration, but its [Fall 2027 nonresident chart](https://www.auburn.edu/administration/finaid/apply/first-year-students.php) lists **ACT 31–32** for Academic Heritage and says competitive awards are not guaranteed. The app mentioned superscoring and non-guarantee but omitted that 27 falls below Heritage's listed range. |
| 2027–28 HOPE | **Cautious and correct for cycle-specific confirmation; incomplete guidance.** | The [GSFC regulations index](https://gsfc.georgia.gov/programs-and-regulations) currently lists 2026–27 as its latest set, so it does not confirm 2027–28 regulations. GSFC's standing [HOPE eligibility page](https://gafutures.org/hope-state-aid-programs/hope-zell-miller-scholarships/hope-scholarship/initial-academic-eligibility/) describes a 3.0 *calculated HOPE GPA* and rigor credits for graduates of eligible high schools, and its [application page](https://gafutures.org/hope-state-aid-programs/hope-zell-miller-scholarships/hope-scholarship/application-and-deadline/) says apply through FAFSA or GSFAPP by the last day of the term or withdrawal date, subject to college requirements. These are current general rules, not a confirmed 2027–28 regulation. The app rightly declined to present 2026–27 regulations as 2027–28, but omitted links and these qualified current-rule pointers. |

**Recording choice:** Georgia Southern is the cleanest example. Park works if the presenter explicitly explains that 3.79 cannot be rounded up under the published rule and that the app has not certified eligibility. Coolidge should not be shown as a correct answer. Alabama/Auburn needs a direct statement that 27 is insufficient for the named Capstone and Academic Heritage score rules before use. HOPE is suitable for demonstrating a refusal to invent 2027–28 rules, while noting the missing official-source handoff.

This was a read-only web verification. No external page was imported into the app or Pinecone, and no paid model query was made for the audit.
