# NextStep Scholarships — five fictional test journeys

Prepared October 14, 2026 for the reviewed Fall 2027 pilot. Use these only after the new reviewed corpus is active and the student app passes its live smoke checks. These are **fictional inputs**, not student records or a claim that an award is open or guaranteed. The homepage fields are optional; leave any field not listed blank. After exploring, select the named award card(s) before asking the question so the chat is scoped to the intended sources. Open a citation and compare its quoted passage, source title, applicable cycle and link with the answer.

## Recommended short recording: 1 normal case + 1 edge case

### 1. Georgia Southern: a normal first-year question

**Homepage:** School year **Senior**; graduation year **2027**; state **GA**; GPA **3.60**, scale **4-point**, type **Unweighted**, documentation **From my school record**; considering colleges **Georgia Southern**.

**Select:** Georgia Southern First-Year Merit Scholarship.

**Ask:** “For a Georgia resident entering Georgia Southern in Fall 2027, is the first-year merit scholarship renewable? Is my 3.60 transcript GPA enough to know the award tier, and when should I complete the admission application?”

**Check:** The cited answer should identify the award as **one-time**, distinguish the university's **recalculated admissions core GPA** from the entered GPA, and explain the **February 1, 2027 priority date**. It should not promise a tier or final eligibility from the entered number. Open the source citation and verify the quote and Fall 2027 label.

### 2. Park Scholarship: a numeric edge case

**Homepage:** School year **Senior**; graduation year **2027**; state **GA**; GPA **3.79**, scale **4-point**, type **Unweighted**, documentation **From my school record**; considering colleges **NC State**.

**Select:** NC State Park Scholarship.

**Ask:** “Park lists a 3.80 unweighted minimum. Can my 3.79 be rounded up? Do I need separate NC State and Park applications, and when are the applications and recommendations due?”

**Check:** The answer should preserve the **non-rounded 3.80** requirement and the separate admission/scholarship paths, cite the relevant official passages, and avoid calling a 3.79 applicant eligible. Other requirements should remain unresolved unless the profile and sources establish them. This is the strongest brief demonstration of an edge case and citations.

## Additional tests

### 3. Coolidge: junior preparation versus senior eligibility

**Homepage:** School year **Junior**; graduation year **2028**; state **GA**. Leave GPA and college fields blank.

**Select:** Coolidge Scholarship.

**Ask:** “I am a junior graduating in spring 2028. Is this the Coolidge cycle for me? What is the deadline for the application and two reference letters, and what should I check before applying?”

**Check:** The answer should connect the published cycle to **Fall 2028 college entry**, give the **December 1, 2026, 5 p.m. Pacific** deadline from the current source, and mention the reference-letter/application requirements. Citizenship or permanent-residence status and other unentered details should remain unknown. It should not reuse these junior rules for a Fall 2027 senior.

### 4. Alabama versus Auburn: similar awards, different score rules

**Homepage:** School year **Senior**; graduation year **2027**; state **GA**; GPA **3.60**, scale **4-point**, type **Unweighted**, documentation **From my school record**; considering colleges **University of Alabama, Auburn**.

**Select:** University of Alabama Capstone and Auburn Academic Heritage Scholarship.

**Ask:** “My ACT superscore is 27, but neither single test date was 27. Could that satisfy Alabama's Capstone score rule? Does Auburn handle scores the same way, and are these awards guaranteed?”

**Check:** The answer should cite Alabama's **single-test-date/no-superscore** rule and Auburn's **superscore** policy. It should distinguish Alabama's automatic tier from Auburn's **competitive consideration**, and avoid treating a score mentioned only in chat as a verified profile fact or promising either award. The homepage has no score field; the score is part of the question only.

### 5. Missing 2027–28 HOPE rules: an honest evidence gap

**Homepage:** School year **Senior**; graduation year **2027**; state **GA**. Leave GPA and colleges blank.

**Select:** No award card; ask from the general chat after exploring.

**Ask:** “What are the confirmed 2027–28 HOPE Scholarship rules for a student entering college in Fall 2027? Can you confirm my required GPA and application deadline?”

**Check:** The app should say the reviewed corpus **does not establish those 2027–28 rules** and direct the user to the official GSFC/GAfutures source. It must not answer with Georgia Southern merit rules or present the **2026–27** HOPE regulation as confirmed for 2027–28. This is a useful short closing example if the recording has time.

## Recording check

Use only one or two journeys in a five-minute recording. A clear sequence is **Case 1** (profile → one award → citation) followed by **Case 2** (3.79 edge → exact 3.80 rule → unresolved requirements). If the missing-source guard passes the final live check, briefly add Case 5 to demonstrate when the app declines to guess. Do not describe these expected behaviors as verified live results until the final app check is recorded.
