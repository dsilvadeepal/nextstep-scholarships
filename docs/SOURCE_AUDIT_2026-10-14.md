# Scholarship source audit — October 14, 2026

**This is the retained first-pass discovery register.** The subsequent second-pass review and admission decisions (historical document removed) supersede its proposed ingestion list. The verified local pilot is now active; historical statements below about the pause/no rebuild describe this first-pass checkpoint.

Scope: Georgia high-school juniors and seniors, and first-year college awards they could pursue at Georgia or neighboring-state universities. This is a **research register**, not an approved ingestion manifest. A linked page is not automatically a complete or current rules source. Dates below are those shown by sponsors; they are not inferred from when a page was fetched. **Application year, high-school graduation year, college-entry year, funding/award year, and regulation effective year are separate fields.** For example, GSFC's 2026–27 PDF is the current published regulation as of this audit, but it does not establish a Fall 2027 entrant's later 2027–28 award rules.

## Source admission rule

For each named award, use the newest official rules that explicitly apply to the student's application or entry cycle. Record the sponsor, award, year, first-year applicability, residence and school restrictions, mandatory requirements, exclusions, application path, separate materials, deadline/time zone, and exact evidence location. Look at the preceding cycle only if no current rules exist, and label those facts historical. Do not use announcements of winners, donor stories, general financial-aid pages, or old PDFs to assert current eligibility. Do not turn selective criteria or past-recipient averages into mandatory thresholds.

Status legend: **rules located** = an official page or PDF states material criteria and application path for an identified cycle, but field-level extraction still requires review; **partial** = official material exists but one or more material items remain unresolved; **historical** = only prior-cycle rules found; **discovery** = a directory or broad page that needs program-level links; **exclude** = unrelated to first-year scholarships or only an award announcement.
